Overview

Twenty-two algorithmic strategies across Solana MEV, Gains gTrade perpetuals, Uniswap spot, and cross-chain arbitrage. Built in Rust and Go with shared crates for execution routing, market-data collection, Chronos forecasting, and portfolio hedging. Each strategy documents thesis, architecture, mathematics and logic, failures, corrections, sample code, and audit logs.
