Signal

This package provides shared infrastructure and historical context for Chronos directional_follow strategies on Gains gTrade. The two arms target different time horizons on related SOL exposure: degen-fast at 1s poll with h=1, swing at 30s poll with h=3. Both can share one Chronos sidecar process.

Mathematics and Logic

Chronos2 emits a one-step expected return estimate r_hat at horizon index h. The execution router treats sign(r_hat) as trade direction and gates size with hysteresis:

Hysteresis reduces flip-churn: enter on weaker evidence, exit only on stronger counter-evidence. Calibration uses the tail of |r_hat| from dryrun audit NDJSON — not blind median percentiles when the distribution is bimodal (noise floor ~1e-6 to 1e-5 vs signal tail ~5e-5 to 1e-4 at 30s cadence).

Shared state machine

Umbrella for degen-fast (h=1, 1s) and chronos-swing (h=3, 30s). Capital must be isolated per arm.

Status and Outcome

Live as infrastructure. Individual arms have their own status (both currently halted pending post-mortem fixes). The shared infrastructure (sidecar, calibration scripts, policy definitions) remains active and serves as the foundation for any future Chronos directional arms.

Failures and Corrections

Key lesson from Jun 18 session: do not run both arms live on one wallet without capital split. The shared-wallet failure was an amplifier that converted swing's flip-churn drawdown into a depleted-collateral problem for degen. See individual arm post-mortems for detailed failure analysis.
