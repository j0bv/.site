Signal

Chronos2 r_hat at horizon index 3 with 40-tick history. London-validated thresholds: OPEN at 2.5e-4, FLIP at 7.0e-4. The bot enters on bullish/bearish threshold crossing and exits on the opposite signal flip after minimum hold period.

No static take-profit; profit-lock scalp is disabled (directional_profit_lock_r = 0). The only non-signal exit is the keeper disaster SL set at open.

Mathematics and Logic

Chronos2 emits a one-step expected return estimate r_hat at horizon index h. The execution router treats sign(r_hat) as trade direction and gates size with hysteresis:

Hysteresis reduces flip-churn: enter on weaker evidence, exit only on stronger counter-evidence. Calibration uses the tail of |r_hat| from dryrun audit NDJSON — not blind median percentiles when the distribution is bimodal (noise floor ~1e-6 to 1e-5 vs signal tail ~5e-5 to 1e-4 at 30s cadence).

Swing calibration (London-validated)

100x leverage on ~600 notional implies ~0.7 round-trip fee per flip — churn dominates if |r_hat| oscillates around threshold.

Status and Outcome

Halted after Jun 18 2026 session. This arm caused the largest single-session drawdown: equity dropped from ~77% (relative) due to flip-churn. 21 opens against 59 force-exit events in one session.

Flip-churn policy review and bounded flip-rate verification required before any live re-enable.

Failures and Corrections

1. Flip-churn: the primary failure mode

Pattern observed: open position, weak opposite r_hat after min-hold (~5.5 min at 30s poll), mandatory full exit via directional_force_exit, immediate re-open on next signal. Each cycle paid ~0.1% round-trip fees. With 21 opens and 59 force-exit events, the account bled from fees without any large adverse market move.

Root cause: This is policy behavior, not a bug. The swing arm uses bot flip as primary exit (unlike degen entry-only). Weak counter-signals after minimum hold trigger mandatory exit, and the next tick's signal re-opens. The threshold calibration was London-validated but the execution policy had no churn rate bound.

Fix required: Bounded flip rate — no open-close cycle within N ticks unless |r_hat| > FLIP sustained. Review protective_exit_sized count per hour in dryrun audit before live.

2. Sequential wallet drain (shared context)

Swing drew down first on the shared wallet, then degen-fast continued on the depleted collateral. Not a correlated market loss — sequential drain of the same USDC pool.

Fix: One arm per wallet unless explicit capital split is designed and tested.

3. Not the primary blow cause (verified)

Investigated and ruled out: 0x3a23d825 partial close revert (absent from this session), correlated SOL crash (fees dominated, not directional loss), and bad calibration alone (thresholds were London-validated; the churn was an execution policy gap).
