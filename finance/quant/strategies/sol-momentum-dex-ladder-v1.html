Sol Momentum DEX Ladder v1

Open-market Solana spot trending memecoins with 3-tranche ladder and tiered exits

EXPERIMENTAL

Instrument / Architecture

FieldValue
ChainSolana
TargetTrending memecoins on any DEX Screener pool
Entry3-tranche ladder (40% / 35% / 25%)
Exit4-tier ladder (+50%, +100%, +200-300%, moonbag)
Hard stops-20% pre-tier1, -15% trailing post-tier1, liquidity drop, time stop
Risk unitDefault 1.0 SOL

Signal

DEX Screener filter: liquidity >= 50K, market cap 500K-5M, 5m price change >= +10%, 1h price change >= +50%, buy/sell ratio > 1.5, holders >= 200 (optional). Rug screen: mint authority revoked, freeze authority revoked, LP lock verification.

Entry tranches: A fires on filter pass, B on 5-10% pullback from post-A high, C on new high above A entry (skip if A+B already profitable).

Mathematics and Logic

Filter score (conjunctive gates)

Ladder entry (tranches of risk unit R)

Tiered exit
