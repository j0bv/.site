Signal

Chronos2 produces a joint forecast panel for BTC, ETH, and SOL. The hedger-core penalized log-growth optimizer yields target fractions x* under per-leg caps and gross leverage limits. Execution-router maps delta (x* - x_curr) to venue orders with anti-churn guards (rebalance cooldown, min-delta threshold, in-flight open lock).

Status and Outcome

Archived — pivoted to single-symbol directional follow (SOLDEGEN first) for small-book deployment. The full hedger stack is preserved for larger-capital configurations. This package documents legacy behavior for portfolio export and intent replay.

Failures and Corrections

1. BTC concentration from venue minimums

best_feasible_signal plus venue minimum notional (~800 at venue minimum) forced BTC opens while ETH/SOL targets were skipped as min_delta_skipped. On a small book, the optimizer's unconstrained target fractions hit venue floor constraints on the non-BTC legs, creating unintended single-asset concentration.

2. Partial shrink held (stuck position)

chronos_reduce_suppressed locked a long BTC position while r_hat and x* had already moved. The reduce-suppression logic (designed to prevent premature exit) interacted badly with multi-leg rebalancing where the optimizer wanted to rotate out of BTC into another leg.

3. Double-open incident

Trades 28/29 in one session exposed a snapshot vs trade-index settlement gap. Two opens fired in sequence because the position snapshot used for the second trade had not yet reflected the first trade's settlement. Anti-churn in-flight lock was the intended guard but failed at the snapshot timing boundary.

Decision

The hedger's complexity (joint optimizer, multi-leg venue constraints, reduce-suppression interactions) exceeded what could be validated on a small book. Pivot to single-symbol directional follow eliminated venue minimum constraints, multi-leg coordination bugs, and reduce-suppression interactions while preserving the core Chronos signal value.
