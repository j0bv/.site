Signal

Playwright bridge sidecar loads DexScreener screener in headless Chromium, intercepts WebSocket frames from the screener, and exposes localhost SSE. The Rust screener consumes SSE (never calls DexScreener directly). Fallback: GeckoTerminal trending_pools REST when bridge is unhealthy.

Trending filter is distinct from cradle: minimum liquidity 25K, minimum 24h transactions 50, minimum 5m price change +5%, pair age >= 1h (vs cradle max 1h), market cap 100K-10M.

Mathematics and Logic

Trending vs cradle

Same pipeline as cradle but inverted age gate: pairs must be established (age >= 1h). Momentum is measured on longer-lived pools; entry uses 5-slice TWAP instead of single bundle snipe.

Status and Outcome

Live with dedicated signer. Discovery sidecar and filter chain validated. Shares exit sidecar binary with launch-mev-sol-v1 but uses distinct trending-specific thresholds (15m hold window, +30% tier1 vs cradle's 30m/+50%).

Failures and Corrections

No documented post-mortem failures. Key differences from cradle that preempt cradle's issues: TWAP entry (5 slices) distributes execution risk vs single bundle; shorter hold window (15m vs 30m) reduces time-in-market exposure; lower tier1 take-profit (+30% vs +50%) captures smaller moves more reliably.
