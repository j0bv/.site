Live in micro-sizing mode. 2074+ lifetime candidates passed screener. Discovery and filter infrastructure validated. Primary bottleneck identified: Jito bundle landing rate at competitive tip levels.

Failures and Corrections

1. Jito landing rate: 0% at initial tip levels

24 micro-sized swap signatures showed 0/24 on-chain landing. Jito accepted the bundles (no rejection), but transactions never appeared on-chain. This is a tip competitiveness issue, not a confirmation-timeout bug.

Root cause: Initial tip was too low for competitive block inclusion in the current Solana fee market (200k lamport era).

Correction: Tip ladder escalated across sessions:

2. Late entry discovery (entry refresh gap)

Screener candidates passed filter at discovery time but by entry submission the pool state had changed. The entry refresh fallback (Gecko then DexScreener then RPC) was implemented to re-validate price before live buy.

Status: Entry refresh fallback validated as working. Rate-limited sources (429) trigger 30s cooldown retry; true dead pools stay permanently skipped.

3. Venue-specific simulation failures

49 simulation failures observed. Meteora DBC (Dynamic Bonding Curve) pools had heavy sim failure rates and zero micro landings.

Fix: Added Meteora DBC to entry DEX blocklist. Simulation pass rate improved on remaining venues (PumpSwap, Pump.fun, Raydium CPMM).

4. Exit sidecar + TWAP close validation

Two pre-micro fills (both on Pump.fun/PumpSwap) were successfully closed via the exit sidecar, validating the tier1 snipe + TWAP close path. Jupiter is the primary execution path for sells on Pump/PumpSwap until direct Pump CPI ships.

5. Variance window and screener tuning

Recent reject reasons dominated by pool_var_too_low (~79%), then liquidity_too_low (~12%). The pool_var_proxy threshold vs pass rate tradeoff is an ongoing calibration lever.
