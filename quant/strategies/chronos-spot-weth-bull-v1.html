Signal

Chronos2 r_hat at horizon index 3 with 30s poll. Sit in USDC when flat. Buy WETH when r_hat clears open threshold (5e-4). Sell to USDC on bearish flip or profit-lock.

Net-edge gate blocks opens where forecast edge is below round-trip cost (pool fee + gas). Success measured as alpha vs buy-and-hold ETH, not raw equity drift.

Mathematics and Logic

Chronos2 emits a one-step expected return estimate r_hat at horizon index h. The execution router treats sign(r_hat) as trade direction and gates size with hysteresis:

Hysteresis reduces flip-churn: enter on weaker evidence, exit only on stronger counter-evidence. Calibration uses the tail of |r_hat| from dryrun audit NDJSON — not blind median percentiles when the distribution is bimodal (noise floor ~1e-6 to 1e-5 vs signal tail ~5e-5 to 1e-4 at 30s cadence).

Status and Outcome

Experimental — conditional go after dryrun #6 validated gated economics. First net-edge gate pass achieved at 30s poll with r_hat ~ 1.1e-3. Not yet live; awaiting confirmation of multiple gate passes across different market windows.

Failures and Corrections

Seven experiment iterations documented the progression from a non-functional configuration to validated economics:

Dryrun #1: Wrong starting inventory

Wallet held ~34% WETH from the predecessor micro-rebalancer era. The bull strategy targets 0% neutral but directional_follow only exits on bearish flip, not inventory drift. Most ticks attempted exit but r_hat was too weak. Net-edge gate was never exercised because the book never reached USDC baseline.

Fix: Flatten wallet to USDC before running bull configuration.

Dryrun #2: Net-edge gate exercised — all blocked

From correct USDC-flat starting point, 8 ticks crossed the open threshold but all were blocked by spot_below_net_edge. At 0.05% pool tier, round-trip pool fees (~0.01% on a ~10 leg) exceeded forecast edge by an order of magnitude. Gate behavior was correct — the economics did not support trading.

Dryrun #3: Diagnostic — signal is active enough

With gate disabled, 35 bullish ticks produced buy intents over 30 minutes. Max r_hat reached 7.1e-4. Signals clustered in momentum bursts (median inter-arrival 10.1s). This proved the signal regime was active; economics were the sole blocker.

Dryrun #4: Pool tier switch insufficient alone

Switched to 0.01% fee tier. Pool cost dropped to ~0.002% round-trip, but gas estimate (200k gas limit) dominated total cost at ~0.012% round-trip. Max r_hat of 5.26e-4 produced edge of ~0.005% — still blocked.

Fix: Calibrate gas limit to observed on-chain swap gas (~127k for legacy router). Updated to 130k.

Dryrun #5: Calibrated gas — still below break-even on quiet session

With 130k gas + 0.01% pool, break-even r_hat is ~1e-3. This session's max was 4.9e-4 — all 10 bullish threshold crosses blocked. The signal can reach 7e-4 (dryrun #3 proved this) but the current session was not volatile enough.

ARB symbol probe: alternative rejected

Tested ARB/USDC as potentially higher-volatility alternative. Native USDC pools on Arbitrum showed stale or near-stale slot0 prices — Chronos received flat input and produced r_hat ~ 1.6e-6. WETH confirmed as the better symbol for this stack.

Dryrun #6: Signal regime change closes the gap

Switched to 30s poll (from 5s). Max r_hat reached 1.1e-3, above the ~1e-3 break-even. One bullish tick produced the first gated pass across all dryruns — a buy leg with no spot_below_net_edge skip. The signal regime change (longer forecast horizon at 30s pace) was the key unlocking economically viable opens.

Current status: Conditional go. Awaiting second observation window with multiple gate passes before live deployment.
