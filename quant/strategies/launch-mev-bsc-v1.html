Launch MEV BSC v1

BSC launch sniping with multi-factory discovery and block-level reserve exits

Instrument / Architecture

FieldValue

Signal

Factory event discovery from PancakeSwap V2, V3, and Four Meme factory contracts. New pool creation events trigger cradle filter. Mandatory pre-submit simulation via Anvil fork (buy then sell cycle) plus GoPlus token safety check in block mode.

Mathematics and Logic

Multi-factory discovery

BSC factories (PancakeSwap V2/V3, Four.meme) emit new pair events. Cradle filter math matches Solana: pool variance, liquidity, mcap bands.

GoPlus risk gate blocks honeypots before simulate. Mark price from on-chain reserve math.

Status and Outcome

Experimental — infrastructure built, not yet in live execution. Discovery limited to three configured factory addresses; launchers outside those are out of scope.

Failures and Corrections

No documented failures — strategy in experimental/pre-live state. Known limitation: explicit discovery gap for factories outside the three configured addresses.
