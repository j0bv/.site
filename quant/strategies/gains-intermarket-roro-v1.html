Mathematics and Logic

RORO thesis

Risk-on/risk-off rotation: QQQ (growth) vs XAU (safe haven). When equity momentum dominates, long QQQ; when fear dominates, long XAU. SPY and FX panel provide regime context.

Phase 1 uses Chronos directional_follow on one or more trade legs. Context instruments (SPY, FX, sector ETFs) inform regime classification; the optimizer ranks candidates with open_leg_selection = intermarket_optimal (Strategy 9 engine in hedger-intermarket).
