Mathematics and Logic

Chronos2 emits a one-step expected return estimate r_hat at horizon index h. The execution router treats sign(r_hat) as trade direction and gates size with hysteresis:

Hysteresis reduces flip-churn: enter on weaker evidence, exit only on stronger counter-evidence. Calibration uses the tail of |r_hat| from dryrun audit NDJSON — not blind median percentiles when the distribution is bimodal (noise floor ~1e-6 to 1e-5 vs signal tail ~5e-5 to 1e-4 at 30s cadence).

Status and Outcome

Archived (Jun 2026). Succeeded by chronos-spot-weth-bull-v1 (USDC baseline). The micro-rebalancer validated infrastructure but failed to achieve the stated goal of bull-run timing capture.

Live session result: +0.3% over ~23.5 hours with 96 on-chain swaps. This was almost entirely ETH beta (underlying moved +0.37%) with negligible timing alpha.

Failures and Corrections

Finding: Passive ETH exposure, not directional timing

The stated goal was "enter/exit WETH on optimal timing for bull runs." Observed behavior: passive half-ETH exposure with micro rebalances. With ~50% WETH at all times, a +0.37% ETH move explains most of the +0.3% return. The bot did not demonstrate bull-run capture — it maintained neutral inventory and churned ~43 notional across 96 swaps averaging ~0.45 each.

Root cause: The 50/50 inventory target means the strategy is structurally a passive holder with micro-rebalancing noise, not a directional timing tool.

Correction: Forked to chronos-spot-weth-bull-v1 with inventory_target_weth_frac = 0.0 (USDC baseline) so bullish signals add WETH exposure intentionally rather than maintaining passive half-ETH.

Infrastructure issues (fixed during session)

Wrong pool address in config (contract had no bytecode on Arbitrum) — 96 historical "swaps" were actually no-op transactions. Also experienced a false drawdown halt when RPC 429 caused equity read to drop to near-zero, triggering the monitor incorrectly.

Fixes: Correct pool address deployed; drawdown monitor hardened against RPC equity glitches.
