Signal

Chronos2 r_hat at horizon index 1 (fastest) drives entry direction. The bot submits an open with keeper TP+SL set at open time. After open, the bot is entry-only — contract keeper orders (oracle-triggered) are the sole exit path.

Fee-aware SL math ensures the stop-loss accounts for the ~0.1% open/close fees at high leverage.

Mathematics and Logic

Chronos2 emits a one-step expected return estimate r_hat at horizon index h. The execution router treats sign(r_hat) as trade direction and gates size with hysteresis:

Hysteresis reduces flip-churn: enter on weaker evidence, exit only on stronger counter-evidence. Calibration uses the tail of |r_hat| from dryrun audit NDJSON — not blind median percentiles when the distribution is bimodal (noise floor ~1e-6 to 1e-5 vs signal tail ~5e-5 to 1e-4 at 30s cadence).

Status and Outcome

Halted after Jun 18 2026 session. Equity drawn down ~65% during the session (shared wallet with chronos-swing-v1). Kill-switch active; dryrun gate required before any live re-enable.

The strategy is mechanically sound post-fix but requires capital isolation (one arm per wallet) and bounded flip-rate verification before re-deployment.

Failures and Corrections

1. Bot flip-close vs keeper (pre-fix)

The bot issued directional_force_exit while keeper TP/SL orders were active on-chain. A phantom position state (x_curr_frac ~ 79 after open with x_star = 0) drove flip-close intent, creating rapid open-close cycles that bled fees without capturing any directional move.

Root cause: The bot set was_in_position on submit before chain confirmation, then attempted to exit a position the keeper was already managing.

Fix: Block flip-close when had_keeper_tp_at_open; do not set position state on submit before chain confirm.

2. Phantom position / stale snapshot

Post-fix restart carried a phantom short position from the previous session. One successful flip-close on the first trade at startup before entry-only logic engaged.

Root cause: Stale snapshot from prior session persisted across restart without chain verification.

Fix: Registry-backed exit sizing; clear stale snapshot on restart; verify chain flat before trading.

3. Fee bleed at ~500x leverage

Seven rapid opens in one session with approximately 0.1% round-trip fee per cycle at ~3k notional. Scratch cycles (no directional gain) dominate PnL on a small book. At this leverage, even a few churned cycles consume a meaningful percentage of collateral.

Status: Structural to the degen lane at high leverage. Mitigation requires bounded open frequency and minimum signal confidence before entry.

4. Dual-bot wallet amplifier

Chronos Swing locked collateral concurrently with degen on the same wallet. Not a correlated market loss — sequential drain on shared USDC as swing drew down first, then degen continued on depleted collateral.

Fix: One arm per wallet, or explicit capital governor with reserved collateral per arm.
